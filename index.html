About

About Me

Politician and consultant with a passion for Frankfurt and financial markets

As a native Frankfurter, I combine political conviction with professional expertise in financial regulation.

Since 2020, I have been a member of the FDP Frankfurt, engaged in various committees of the Frankfurt Römer with a focus on digitization, economy, and education.

With over 15 years of experience in the financial industry, I specialize in regulatory issues, compliance, and risk management for banks and fintechs.
